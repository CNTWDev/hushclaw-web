Quick Start

Install

Uninstall

Keep data bash <(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CNTWDev/hushclaw/master/uninstall.sh) --keep-data

Our philosophy

Models change. Your context shouldn't.

New models and agents arrive every month, and your work is scattered across them. HushClaw gives your preferences, background and experience one place to accumulate, so every model you use starts from what you've already taught it.
